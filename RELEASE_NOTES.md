Increases the game's existing recruitment-rating bonus for transfer-listed or transfer-requesting players from **5% to 35%** (1.05 to 1.35).

Download the [patch package ZIP](https://github.com/jasperkoningnl/cm0102-increased-interest/archive/refs/heads/main.zip), extract it and read README.md in the extracted folder. Back up your executable and run **CheckCompatibility.cmd** before installing through Nick's Patcher.

The author reports a clear increase in interest and bids over multiple transfer windows, with the trade-off of sometimes receiving a lot of bids. The patch affects players at both human and AI clubs and can affect offer valuations. It does not guarantee bids or completed sales.

For compatible expanded 3.9.68 executables only. Bosman Edition and unexpanded executables are not supported. Includes a conservative read-only compatibility checker, removal patch, technical notes and test notes. All 13 release-checker cases passed; installation/removal round trips restored the original bytes.

No game executable, database or save is included. Independent community beta. Feedback through Issues is welcome.
